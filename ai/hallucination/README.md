## Deep dive

Hallucination is unsupported or incorrect generated content. Reduce risk through better retrieval, constrained outputs, citations, verification, tool use, and calibrated refusal behavior, but do not treat any technique as a truth guarantee. Distinguish model fluency from evidence-backed correctness.

### Interview and implementation drill
Explain the threat or optimization model, implement a small example, identify failure modes, and connect the solution to production observability and evaluation.
