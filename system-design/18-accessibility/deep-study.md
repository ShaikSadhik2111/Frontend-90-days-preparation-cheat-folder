# Accessibility Architecture

## Why it matters
Build accessibility into component and system contracts. Cover semantic HTML, accessible names, keyboard navigation, focus management, ARIA, live regions, reduced motion, contrast and error messaging. Interview drill: design an accessible combobox or modal. Challenge: audit a complex project feature with keyboard-only interaction and screen-reader semantics.

## Study contract
Explain the design decision, the failure model, the measurement strategy and the trade-offs. Be able to defend the choice without relying on a framework name.

## Interview follow-ups
- What fails first at 10× scale?
- What is the recovery path?
- What signal would prove the system is healthy?
- What security or accessibility risk remains?

## Practical output
Add a measured experiment, threat model, failure matrix or ADR to the project.
