> Preparation is useless without interview calls. Treat the job search as a second "project" with daily tasks (they're in the plan).

## Naukri (most MNC recruiters in India search here)
- Headline: "Frontend Engineer | React, TypeScript, AngularJS → React migration | 4.5 yrs | Bosch".
- Key skills: add 15–20 keywords from above; profile summary same as resume.
- **Update something on the profile every 1–2 days** (keeps you higher in recruiter searches).
- Expected CTC: set it a little **above** your target (e.g. 25–27 LPA) to leave room to negotiate. Notice period: be accurate; if you can negotiate early release / buy-out, say so.
- Apply to 5–10 relevant roles per day from Day 7 of the plan. Search titles: "Senior Frontend Developer", "Senior Software Engineer – Frontend", "UI Developer React", "Software Engineer III Frontend", "Lead UI Engineer".

## LinkedIn
- Headline like Naukri; "Open to work" → recruiters only.
- About section: 4–5 lines of the story + skills.
- Featured: GitHub repo + a short post about "Lessons from migrating AngularJS to React" (this post alone can bring recruiter messages).
- Connect with 10 engineers/recruiters per day at target companies with a short note.

## Referrals (highest conversion)
Message template:
> Hi __, I'm a frontend engineer at Bosch (4.5 yrs, React/TypeScript, led an AngularJS → React migration). I saw the __ role (Job ID __) at __. Would you be open to referring me? Resume attached — happy to share more. Thank you!

Keep it short, attach resume + job link, follow up once after 4–5 days.

## Interview cadence
- Weeks 3–4: 2–3 **calibration** interviews at companies that are not your top choice.
- Weeks 5–8: target companies. Schedule top choices after you've done at least 3 real interviews.
- After every interview: write down every question within 1 hour → `interview/my-interview-log.md` → study the gaps.
